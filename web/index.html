    What Cordon does not do

    1. It does not prove Paxos' reserves. The reserve figure is KPMG's, posted by us with the report's hash so anyone can check it.
    2. It does not see other chains. A problem minted on Ethereum or Solana is not in these checks.
    3. It cannot see contract upgrades; USDG exposes no way to read them on chain.
    4. It does not protect money held in USDG. It protects people who accept USDG for something else.
    5. It never blocks repaying, withdrawing or liquidating.
    6. No key we hold can cause a HALT. Only a supply jump read from the chain can. Checked by 5 symbolic proofs and 6 random-sequence tests on every change.
    7. Someone holding about a quarter of the chain's USDG can trip HALT by bridging it out and back in. The discount a market picks limits what that costs borrowers.
    8. A large legitimate move, such as a big bridge-in, also trips HALT. The owner can release it only after six hours of public notice, and only if supply has not grown since.
